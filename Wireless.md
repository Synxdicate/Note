## NOMA

| Technique       | Separated By         | Simultaneous TX? | Complexity | Era/Usage            |
| --------------- | -------------------- | ---------------- | ---------- | -------------------- |
| **FDMA**        | Frequency            | Yes              | Low        | 1G Mobile, FM Radio  |
| **TDMA**        | Time slots           | No (takes turns) | Medium     | 2G GSM               |
| **CDMA**        | Code                 | Yes              | High       | 3G                   |
| **OFDMA**       | Orthogonal sub-freq  | Yes              | High       | 4G LTE, WiFi 6       |
| **SDMA**        | Space/Direction      | Yes              | Very High  | 5G Massive MIMO      |
| **NOMA**        | Power level          | Yes              | Very High  | 5G/6G Research       |
| **ALOHA**       | Random chance        | Sometimes        | Very Low   | Early IoT, Satellite |
| **CSMA/CA**<br> | Listen + random wait | Rarely           | Low-Medium | WiFi, ZigBee         |
